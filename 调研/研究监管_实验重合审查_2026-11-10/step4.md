# Step 4 — High-Potential Candidates

Timestamp: 2026-11-10 (Asia/Shanghai)

1. **DiffuTrack** — closest overall framework: box distribution, online association, short occlusion/gap handling, and association-centric evaluation.
2. **DiffMOT** — direct generative-motion MOT precedent and the strongest threat to any claim framed merely as replacing KF/diffusion with FM.
3. **DMMTracker** — directly threatens the claim that identity-discriminative motion prediction is new.
4. **TrajE** — early direct precedent for MDN/multiple motion hypotheses inside MOT; makes MDN a mandatory baseline.
5. **Constraint-Aware Flow Matching** — same broad mechanism/insight of sending downstream decision constraints into FM training, though outside MOT.
6. **TrajFlow** — FM, multiple candidates and ranking/utility supervision; different task because agent identity is already known.
7. **DOA-SORT** — same failure regime and distributional association, but it models observation bias rather than the latent motion distribution.

OA-SORT, MOTIP/MOTIP2, Deep Network Flow and FAMNet remain important contextual prior art but were not promoted to the seven-paper full-text comparison because their main mechanisms are alternative explanations or generic differentiable association rather than the claimed combined mechanism.
